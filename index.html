Projects

Self-Assessment Tool, live client project Business analysis · Agile · Figma · CakePHP · MongoDB +
Featured: client work, delivered live

The need

BIG-CAT runs an online self-assessment platform that helps NDIS participants build independence skills, and gives occupational therapists a report to track their progress. The business owners wanted to increase user engagement and retention, streamline the user journey and improve cybersecurity in line with NDIS requirements, with the long-term aim of running the business with little day-to-day effort.

My role

Business Analyst and team coordinator in a team of five, working directly with the client across two iterations from August to October 2025.

What I did

  • Ran the analysis of needs at the start of each iteration to capture the client's goals, pain points and expectations
  • Turned those goals into a prioritised feature list across three priority levels, with the business value of each feature written in plain language
  • Documented personas for the people using the system: participants, guardians, individuals and occupational therapists
  • Produced the Business Vision and Build 2 Analysis and Design reports with the team, and the user stories and Figma wireframes behind them
  • Planned each iteration around a presentation, client acceptance testing and sign-off, and reduced the risk of delays by booking regular client meetings in advance
  • Supported client acceptance testing, training and handover documentation

What we delivered

  • Iteration 1: a free trial of the system, tracking of when users drop off, and a clearer homepage that directs therapists, guardians and individuals to the right place
  • Iteration 2: more value in the free report to encourage upgrades, participant progress tracking, automated engagement emails and a reusable library of ad components

What changed because of testing

Testing showed that some buttons weren't working properly and some colours were hard to read. We fixed the broken buttons, adjusted the colours and tidied the layout before going live.

Outcome

The tool went live on the client's own hosting server. At the Build 2 presentation the client was fully satisfied with the deliverables and asked for no further revisions, and signed off in October 2025.
